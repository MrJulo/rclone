Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purpose

This application uses rclone to access and manage my own Google Drive files for personal use.

Google Drive Access

The application requests Google Drive permissions to read, upload, modify, and delete files as directed by the account owner.

Data Storage

This website does not collect Google Drive files. OAuth credentials are stored in the user's configured rclone environment.

Data Sharing

Files may be transferred between Google Drive and computing environments controlled by the account owner. Personal data is not sold to advertisers.

Data Control

Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account security settings.
